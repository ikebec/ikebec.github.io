About

This is a one-man operation focused on the repair and sale of acoustic flat-top guitars. I collect and restore older Martin guitars, as I am a fan of the makers musical history, design and build. I enjoy the process of repairing these guitars and aim to get the instruments leaving my shop ready for years of play ahead. When working on guitars I generally use traditional materials and hand techniques with the goal being quality, lasting and honest craftsmanship.
